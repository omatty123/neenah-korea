Where the parallels run

Different histories, familiar instincts

Water / Nature / 4 Seasons

Wisconsin: 15,000+ lakes. Fishing, ice shanties, lake cabins. Brutal winters, lush summers. Water and weather shape the calendar and the identity.

Korea: 70% mountains, watersheds everywhere. Sea on three sides. Four sharp seasons deeply embedded in culture, food, and holidays. Nature is not backdrop, it is structure.

Gemütlichkeit / Jeong (정)

German-heritage word for cozy communal warmth. Untranslatable. You feel it at a supper club, a fish boil, a Packers tailgate. Highest binge-drinking rate in the US. The tavern is a social institution.

Korean word for deep emotional bonds built through shared time. Untranslatable. You feel it at a family meal, a late night with coworkers. Soju is the best-selling spirit on Earth. 건배 means "dry the glass."

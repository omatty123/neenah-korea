The future runs through South Korea.

AI, factories & ships

Korean companies supply the high-bandwidth memory that feeds data to AI processors. In 2026, Samsung and SK hynix began shipping the next generation: HBM4.

Sources: Samsung, February 2026 · SK hynix, July 2026

Poor does not mean powerless.

Russia, brands & daily life

Russia’s partnership brings new resources. The 20×10 policy is adding regional factories and services. State-owned firms also develop brands and compete for customers. State ownership and market activity can coexist. More cars now bring traffic jams to Pyongyang. See the videos and evidence.

The Bank of Korea estimated that North Korea’s economy grew 3.5% in 2025. Its ability to mobilize resources deserves attention. That does not establish better living conditions.

Source: Bank of Korea, July 2026

Korea is closer to Wisconsin than you think.

One fact

South Korea did not sign the 1953 armistice.

The war paused. It never ended. There is no peace treaty. The Korean War is technically still ongoing, 73 years later.

Wisconsin Medal of Honor recipients Korean War

Cpl. Mitchell Red Cloud Jr.

Ho-Chunk Nation · Hatfield, WI · Nov. 5, 1950

Detected roughly 1,000 Chinese troops approaching his hilltop position at 3:20 AM. Sounded the alarm and engaged them alone with his BAR at point-blank range. Shot multiple times in the chest, he refused evacuation, had himself propped against a tree, and kept firing until he was killed. His stand gave his company time to consolidate and repel the attack. He was 25.

Sgt. Einar H. Ingman Jr.

Milwaukee / Tomahawk, WI · Feb. 26, 1951

Took command of two squads during the Third Battle of Wonju. Charged an enemy machine-gun position and destroyed it, then attacked a second. Hit by grenade fragments and gunfire that tore through his face and neck, he still killed the entire gun crew before falling unconscious. Returned to Tomahawk and worked 32 years at a paper company.

# DPRK brand gallery

LOCAL assets for the Neenah Korea talk website. All eight images were visually inspected on 4 October 2026. These are photographs and an official photographic product montage, with brand or producer identification checked against visible labels and source captions. No AI images were used.

## Original files and display copies

The JPEG and PNG files preserve the complete source photographs. Newspaper JPEGs were extracted directly from the cited PDFs. The Tumangang source JPEG2000 bitstream is retained as `tumangang-beer-2025-source.jp2`; its complete decoded image is also retained as PNG. The WebP display files use quality 84, or 88 for the two smaller cosmetics photographs. Only Tumangang was resized, from 2385 × 1689 to 1800 × 1275. No image was cropped or retouched. Original publication watermarks remain visible.

## Source dates and naming

The dates below are publication dates unless explicitly described otherwise. A 2025 article does not establish that its photograph was taken in 2025. The two cosmetics assets have 2024 upload paths; their exact capture dates are not stated. Tumangang is from an official product catalog, not an independently observed retail transaction. The regional food images identify producers and package designs; they do not establish sales totals or equal retail availability across the country.

Paegunsan and Jangjasan are the producer names visible at the exhibition, not invented names for individual snacks. Ryuwon is established by the caption and article about that factory. This collection does not claim a separately verified Songdo photograph.

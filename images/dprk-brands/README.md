These files are real Korean Central Television (KCTV) frames reproduced in Martyn Williams and Iliana Ragnone, [“20×10 Factories: Different Locations, Similar Products,” 38 North, June 6, 2025](https://www.38north.org/2025/06/20x10-factories-different-locations-similar-products/).

- Suggested caption: **Kujang Daily Necessities Factory: different packages for rose, honeysuckle, mugwort and citrus soaps. KCTV, reproduced by 38 North, June 2025.**
- Suggested alt: **Visitors inspect differently colored soap packages at Kujang Daily Necessities Factory, with signs naming four scents.**
- The article identifies the scents and explains that the regional factories use different packaging designs. The photo's signs visibly distinguish rose, honeysuckle, mugwort and citrus soap. It is evidence of differentiated products and packaging; it does not establish sales volume or household purchasing power.
